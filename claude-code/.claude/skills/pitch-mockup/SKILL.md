# Make a pitch mockup

A pitch needs two pictures: the business's first screen as a customer sees it on a phone, with the leak circled, and the same first screen fixed, in their brand. The owner looks at them side by side and sees the problem and the fix without reading a word.

## Picture one: the proof
1. Open their homepage in the phone view (CLAUDE.md, Phone view), and let it settle. Take the first screen exactly as it is: the cookie wall, the slider, whatever covers it.
2. Circle the leak in red: the missing call button's spot, the slider where the promise should be, the buried number. One circle, or two at most. A label in two or three words beside each, the leak's label from the audit ("Number buried", "Nothing clear").
3. Use what the audit found when there is one (the skill "Audit a local business's lead funnel" (lead-leak-audit)); without one, check the first screen yourself against the skill "The first screen that converts" (first-screen) and circle the worst leak.

## Picture two: the fix
1. The same phone frame, the same business, the first screen as the skill "The first screen that converts" (first-screen) says: their logo top left, a call button top right with their real number, one line with the trade and the town and a promise, the book button, their real Google rating and count, one real photo of theirs or a plain brand color.
2. In their brand: their logo and colors from the current site or their Facebook page. No logo: their name in a plain bold font.
3. Everything on it is true. Their real number, their real rating from Google Maps, their real services. The promise is a placeholder the owner replaces, marked plainly ("Same day service" in brackets) until the owner gives theirs.
4. Build it as one HTML page at a phone width (390 pixels), with their logo file and real details, and screenshot it in the phone view (CLAUDE.md, Phone view). Save it beside the proof as mockups/<business>/fix.png.

## Deliver
1. Two pictures in the chat, proof first, fix second, each with one line: "Their first screen today: the number is three taps away." and "The same screen with the call button, the rating and one promise."
2. One picture with both side by side too, when the user will paste it into an email.
3. Nothing else: no advice, no price. The pitch email is written separately (CLAUDE.md, Who fixes what).

## Never
Never contact the business, never invent a rating or a review, and never put a competitor's name on the mockup.
