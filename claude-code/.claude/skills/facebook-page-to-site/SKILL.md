# Turn a Facebook page into a website

Many trades businesses have only a Facebook page. It has what a site needs: the name, the number, the hours, the area, the services in the posts, photos of real jobs and recommendations. This skill turns it into the gather step of the skill "Build a converting site" (build-converting-site).

## Read the page
1. Open the page in the browser (not WebFetch): Facebook draws itself with scripts, and some of it needs scrolling. Never sign in as the business, and never message the page.
2. From About: the name as they write it, the phone, the address or area, the hours, the website if any, the year started, the categories.
3. From the posts, the last two years: the services they actually do (what they post about), the towns they name, the promises they repeat, the words they use for themselves. Write the services as a list with a line each, in their words.
4. From Photos: the ten best real photos of finished work, the team and the truck. Save them at full size. Skip memes, shared posts and anything with other people's logos.
5. From Reviews or Recommendations: the count, and three to quote with first names. The Google listing's rating and count come from Google Maps, not Facebook.
6. The logo: the profile picture when it is a logo, the cover photo when it holds one. Note the two or three colors they use.

## What Facebook cannot give
Ask the owner for these, in one message, before you build: the one promise they will keep (same day, a window, a price), the licence or insurance line, and whether the number on the page is the one that rings.

## Then build
Follow the skill "Build a converting site" (build-converting-site) with what you gathered. On the site, the Facebook page is one small link in the footer, and the Facebook page gets, in the owner's hands, a Website button pointing at the new site and a pinned post saying it is up.

## Show it
Before the full build, show the owner the first screen next to their Facebook page's top on a phone, so they see the difference: build the first screen as one HTML page from the first screen rule, in their colors and with their real rating, and screenshot both in the phone view (CLAUDE.md, Phone view).

## Never
Never copy a photo that is not theirs, never quote a recommendation they did not get, and never post, message or change anything on Facebook yourself.
