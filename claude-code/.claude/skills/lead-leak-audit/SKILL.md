# Audit a local business's lead funnel

Find where one local business loses customers, on a phone, in the order a customer meets them. The result is a short report with proof, not advice.

## Before you start
1. You need a name and a town, or a website. With only a trade and a town, search Google Maps for the trade and the town in the browser (or WebSearch) for the candidates, then audit each in turn, or the top five when there are many.
2. Never contact the business: no form submissions, calls, texts or emails to a prospect. A test call or a test form goes only to a signed client who asked for it.
3. Do it in a browser in the phone view (CLAUDE.md, Phone view), and read every page as a phone shows it. WebFetch alone misses what covers the first screen. Save each screenshot under audits/<business>/.

## 1. Website, first screen
Load the homepage on the phone view and stop before any scroll. Note what covers the screen. Score each: Number buried (no tap to call button), No way to book (no book or quote button), Rating hidden (no Google rating), Nothing clear (no one line saying trade and area), No reason today (no time promise or offer), First screen wasted (a slider, stock photo, cookie wall or menu where the button should be), Slow to load (over 3 seconds, or things jump). Take a screenshot of the first screen as proof.

## 2. Website, the rest
One tap each: the call button dials, the form submits (do not submit it), the services page exists, the pages say the towns served. If they run ads (step 4), check that the landing page repeats the ad's offer: Ad says one thing.

## 3. Google listing
Search the business's name on Google Maps on the phone view. Count reviews in the last 90 days and compare with the two or three leaders for the same trade in that town (search the trade and the town). Score: Reviews falling behind, No owner replies (last reply older than a month, or none), Thin profile (missing services, hours, area, description, booking link, or no photo newer than a year), Wrong details (phone, address or hours differ from the website).

## 4. Google Ads
Search the trade and the town, then the business's own name, on the phone view. Score: No offer in the ad (their ad only names them), Rival on their name (a competitor's ad above them on their own name), Ad to the homepage (the ad lands on the homepage, not a page about the service).

## 5. Follow up
Only for a signed client who asked: call outside hours and send the form once, then score Missed calls lost (no text back within a minute), Forms go cold (no reply within an hour), No review ask (no request after a job). For a prospect, mark these Not checked.

## The report
Write it in this order, nothing else:
1. Biggest leak: one label, one line of proof (what you saw, where, when).
2. The next two, the same way.
3. Every other leak found, labels only, one line.
4. What was fine, in one line.
5. The first screen screenshot.
Say the leaks you saw, never ones you guess. A leak you could not check is Not checked, with why. No fixes here: the fix belongs to the agent that owns the leak (CLAUDE.md, Who fixes what).
