# Check the Google Ads leaks

When a business pays for search ads, three leaks waste the money: an ad with no offer, an ad that lands on the homepage, and a rival's ad above them on their own name. Check from the outside first; touch the account only with the owner's word.

## From the outside
1. In the phone view (CLAUDE.md, Phone view), search the trade and the town. Read the ads: is theirs there, and does it make an offer (a price, a time promise, a discount with an end date), or only name them? Score No offer in the ad.
2. Tap their ad once, no more: where does it land? The homepage, or a page about the service? Does the page repeat the offer? Score Ad to the homepage, and Ad says one thing for the site.
3. Search their own name. Is a competitor's ad above them? Score Rival on their name. Take a screenshot as proof.
4. Do the three searches at two times of day: ads come and go with budgets.

## The smallest fix first
1. An offer in the headline, the same offer on the landing page's first line (the skill "The first screen that converts" (first-screen)). Draft the headline and the first line in the owner's words and show them.
2. One landing page per service the ads sell, each passing the first screen check, with the form (the skill "Build a trades intake form" (trades-intake-form)). Draft the first screen of each before building any.
3. A rival on their name: a small campaign on their own name, with an ad that says it is them, a sitelink to the booking form, and a landing page that says it is them. Say the monthly cost plainly, from the account's own estimate, never a guess.
4. Call extensions on every ad, so the first screen of the ad has the number.

## In the account
Only when the owner has said so, in the browser after they sign in themselves (or add you to the account), or through the Google Ads API with keys from the environment. Read first: search terms, which ads get calls, what the landing pages are. Change one thing at a time, pause nothing without saying what it was doing, and never raise a budget. Every change waits for the owner's OK unless they have told you to just do it.

## Report
In a few lines: the three leaks scored with proof, what each fix is, what it costs if anything, and what you would need (access, a page, an offer from the owner) to do it. Then stop; the owner decides.

## Never
Never click a competitor's ad (it costs them money and reads as fraud). Never run ads for a business that has not signed. Never promise clicks, calls or a rank.
