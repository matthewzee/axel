# Fix a Google Business Profile

For most local trades the Google listing is the real first screen: it shows before the website does. A full, current profile with matching details and recent photos ranks and converts; a thin one leaks.

## Read it first
1. On a phone view, search the business on Google Maps and read everything: name, category, phone, address or service area, hours, website, booking link, description, services, attributes, photos and their dates, reviews and replies, posts, questions.
2. Search the trade and the town and read the two or three businesses above them the same way. Note what they have that this one lacks.
3. Score: Thin profile (what is missing), Wrong details (what differs from the website), No owner replies, Reviews falling behind (last 90 days against the leaders).

## The fixes, in order
1. Details that match the website exactly: name as on the truck, phone, address or service area, hours, website. One set of details everywhere.
2. Primary category: the trade. Secondary categories only for services they really do.
3. Services: every main service listed, each with one plain line in the owner's words. Prices only when the owner wants them shown.
4. Description: 750 characters at most, the trade, the towns, how long, the promise, the licence line. No keyword lists.
5. Booking link: the site's form (the skill "Build a trades intake form" (trades-intake-form)), so Book goes to a lead, not a contact page.
6. Photos: ten recent real ones of finished work, the team and the truck, with the logo as the logo photo and a cover photo of real work. No stock photos.
7. Replies: catch up on every review without one, in the owner's voice (the skill "Run review requests after jobs" (review-requests) says how).
8. Questions: answer the open ones as the owner, plainly.

## How to change it
The owner's Google account manages the profile. Work in the browser after the owner signs in themselves (or adds you as a manager), never with a password in the chat, and change one section at a time. Every change waits for the owner's OK on the wording unless they have told you to just do it. When the owner has not given access, write every change out for them to paste, in order.

## Report
Before and after, in a few lines: fields filled (count), photos (count and newest date), services listed, replies outstanding, and the two or three leaders' review counts. Then the one thing the owner has to do themselves, if any.

## Never
Never change a prospect's listing, never suggest an edit to one you do not manage, never add a keyword to the business name, and never invent a service, a year or a licence.
